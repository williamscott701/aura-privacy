Privacy Policy

Aura Migraine Diary · Effective 5 September 2026

Aura keeps your diary on your iPhone. It has no account, no server, and makes no network requests of any kind. Nothing you write in it is sent anywhere, including to us — there is nowhere for it to be sent to.

What Aura records, and where it stays

Everything you enter — attacks, severity, symptoms, medications and whether they helped, daily check-ins, cycle phase, and your notes — is stored on your device by iOS's own SwiftData, inside an app group shared only with Aura's own widget extension, so that a widget button can log an attack without opening the app.

We cannot read any of it. Aura contains no networking code, so there is no mechanism by which your diary could reach us even if we wanted it.

Two consequences of it being on your device are worth stating plainly:

  • Your device backups include it. If you back up to iCloud or to a computer, your diary is inside that backup, governed by Apple's terms and your own backup encryption rather than by this policy. That is iOS behaviour and applies to app data generally.
  • Deleting the app deletes the diary. There is no copy anywhere else to restore from. Settings has Export diary, which writes a JSON file you can keep wherever you choose.

What Aura does not do

No account, no sign-up, no email address. No analytics, no telemetry, no usage measurement. No advertising and no ad identifiers. No tracking, and no data shared with data brokers or any other third party. No third-party SDKs of any kind — Aura is built only on Apple's own frameworks. No location. No access to Health, Contacts, Photos, the camera or the microphone.

The App Store privacy label for Aura reads Data Not Collected, and that is meant literally.

Health information

What you record in Aura is health information about you. Because it never leaves your device and we never receive it, we do not process it and are not a controller of it in the sense the GDPR uses. You control it directly: it is on your phone, you can export it, and you can delete all of it from Settings.

Aura is a diary. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose, and it does not give medical advice or treatment recommendations. Its analysis reports what was recorded alongside what, never what caused what.

Your subscription

Aura Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the purchase and tells Aura only whether an active subscription exists for the Apple Account on the device — never a name, an email address, or any payment detail, none of which Aura ever sees or stores.

Apple's own handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's privacy policy, not this one. Manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Notifications

If you turn a reminder on, it is scheduled by iOS on your device and delivered by your device. Nothing about it goes through a server, and Aura sends no push notifications.

Crash reports

There are two, and neither sends anything anywhere by itself.

Apple's crash reporting. If you have left “Share With App Developers” on in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may send us a crash report containing diagnostic information about the crash — a stack trace, device model and OS version. This is collected by Apple under your consent to Apple, and reaches us through Apple's developer tools. You can turn it off in iOS Settings at any time. It contains no diary content.

The report Aura offers you. If a previous run ended unexpectedly, Aura builds a report from a trail of which screens and actions were used, and offers it to you in Settings. It deliberately records no diary content — screen and action names only. Aura never sends it. You read it, and then you choose whether to share it and with whom, using the iOS share sheet. If you send it to us, we use it only to fix the fault and keep it no longer than that takes.

Your rights

Because we hold nothing about you, there is no account to access, correct or close, and no request to us that would produce your data. The equivalent controls are in the app itself:

  • Export — Settings → Export diary writes your whole diary as a JSON file.
  • Erase — Settings → Delete all data removes every attack, dose and check-in from the device. It cannot be undone.
  • Erase everything, including settings — delete the app.

If you are in the EU, the UK, California, or anywhere with comparable law, those rights apply to us too; there is simply nothing held for them to operate on. Write to us if you disagree, and we will answer.

Children

Aura is not directed at children and we knowingly collect nothing from anybody, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version replaces this page and the effective date above changes with it. Because Aura holds no contact details for you, we cannot notify you directly — the current version is always the one at this URL.
